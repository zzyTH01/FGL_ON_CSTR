## 5. Judgment

| Criterion | Value | Threshold (Theory ①) | Threshold (Theory ②) | Verdict |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|------|
| τ_peak range | 3 | ≤ 2 | > 5 | Borderline → ① |
| Offset std | 1.92 | < 2 | > 3 | Supports ① |
| config_D τ_peak vs L+H−1 | 12 vs 9 | Near τ≈13 | Near τ≈9 | Strongly ① |

**Final verdict: SUPPORTS Theory ① (Dynamical Bifurcation).**

The sweet spot at τ≈13 is a property of the Mackey-Glass equation's period-doubling bifurcation, NOT a geometric artifact of the hyperparameters L and H. All four configurations show strong positive FGL in the τ=12~15 region regardless of L+H−1 (which varies from 9 to 16). If geometry were the cause, config_D (L+H−1=9) should have peaked near τ=9, but instead it peaked at τ=12.
